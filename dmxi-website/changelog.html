Version 1.0 Latest Desktop
Released September 2026  ·  Initial public release
The first public release of DMXI Accounting. Includes the full cloud web app at app.dmxiaccounting.com plus a self-contained Windows desktop app (DMXI.exe) that runs entirely on your own computer — no internet, no monthly fees, no PostgreSQL required.
Invoicing & Billing
  • Invoice creation with auto-numbering Sequential invoice numbers (INV-0001, INV-0002…) generated automatically per company.
  • Full & partial payment recording Record one or many payments against an invoice; outstanding balance updates in real time.
  • Shareable invoice links Generate a public URL to send to clients — they see a clean invoice view without needing an account.
  • Credit notes Issue credit notes linked to original invoices with automatic ledger reversal entries.
  • Quotations module Create numbered quotes with validity dates; convert to invoice in one click when accepted.
  • VAT & discount on line items Apply percentage VAT and discount per invoice; totals calculated automatically.
Expenses & Purchases
  • Expense tracking with category codes Log operating costs by account category; filter by vendor, date, or status.
  • Purchase orders with multi-item lines Record supplier bills with voucher numbers (PUR-0001…), multiple product lines, and VAT.
  • File attachments Attach scanned receipts or bills to any expense, purchase, or invoice for a full paperless audit trail.
  • Vendor management Maintain a vendor list with contact details; link all purchases and expenses per supplier.
Accounting & Ledger
  • Double-entry general ledger Every transaction posts balanced DR and CR journal entries automatically. Debit = Credit enforced at all times.
  • Chart of accounts with pre-seeded codes Standard account codes loaded on company creation (Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, Expenses).
  • Manual journal entries Post custom DR/CR entries with memo and reference for adjustments, accruals, or corrections.
  • Account statements & daybook View full transaction history per account or across all accounts for any date range.
Financial Reports
  • Trial Balance All account DR/CR totals side by side; proves double-entry integrity (Σ DR = Σ CR).
  • Profit & Loss statement Revenue vs. expenses for any date range; monthly breakdown chart included.
  • Balance Sheet Assets, liabilities, equity, and retained earnings as of any date — always balanced.
  • Cash Flow report Net change in cash accounts over a period, broken down by counter-account category.
  • Excel & PDF export Every report exports with your company name and logo — ready to hand to your accountant or auditor.
Stock Management
  • Product catalogue with SKUs & brands Manage products with unit price, unit of measure, reorder levels, and brand grouping.
  • Stock count history Record physical stock counts; system auto-calculates variance against expected quantities.
  • Stock movement via purchases Receiving goods on a purchase order automatically updates product stock quantities.
Users & Companies
  • Multi-company support One account can manage unlimited companies; switch between them from the dashboard.
  • Role-based access control Four roles per company: Owner, Admin, Accountant, Viewer — each with distinct permissions.
  • Audit log Every create, update, delete, and cancel action is recorded with the user, timestamp, and changed values.
  • Branch sync Export a branch company's data as a .dmxi bundle and import it into a head-office company — customers, invoices, payments, and expenses all merged.
Desktop App — Windows
  • DMXI.exe — single-file Windows app Download and run — no Node.js, no PostgreSQL, no installation wizard. Everything bundled in one executable.
  • SQLite database — data stays on your PC All data written to a dmxi.db file beside the exe. No cloud, no subscription, no data leaving your computer.
  • Auto-opens browser on launch Double-click DMXI.exe and your browser opens to http://localhost:4000 automatically after 1.5 seconds.
  • Works completely offline No internet connection required after download. Full functionality available offline.
