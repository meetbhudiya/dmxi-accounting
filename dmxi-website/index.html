Everything your books need

Built for accountants who want precision and business owners who want clarity — without two separate systems.

Double-Entry Ledger

Every transaction posts balanced DR and CR entries. Trial Balance, P&L, and Balance Sheet always reconcile — no manual checking.

Invoicing

Create numbered invoices, record full or partial payments, and track outstanding receivables by client at a glance.

Purchases

Log supplier bills with voucher numbers, track what's paid and what's outstanding, and attach scanned receipts for a full audit trail.

Expenses

Categorise operating costs by account code, set up recurring entries for rent and utilities, and export any period to PDF or Excel.

Financial Reports

Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, daybook, and account statements — with one-click Excel or PDF export branded with your company name.

Stock Management

Track inventory by SKU, record movements in and out, and generate stock valuation reports with cost of goods sold analysis.

Set up in minutes

No accounting firm required to get started. Add your company, chart of accounts is pre-seeded with standard codes, and start entering transactions.

1
Create your company

Register, add a company name and base currency. Your chart of accounts is pre-loaded with standard account codes ready to use.

2
Enter transactions

Record income, expenses, purchases, and manual journal entries. Attach receipts or bills to any transaction for paperless record-keeping.

3
Run your reports

Pull Trial Balance, P&L, or Balance Sheet for any date range. Export with your company name to hand to your accountant or auditor.
